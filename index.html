BEFORE YOU ASK

A few practical
questions.

It’s okay to check the details first.

What STEM classes does The Tins offer?+

The Tins offers offline STEM classes in coding and embedded engineering with Vedant Kumar in Delhi. We also offer real-world skills classes with Amritansh Dubey and online tuition with Zeenat Khan.

Who teaches the classes?+

Vedant Kumar teaches coding and embedded engineering. Amritansh Dubey teaches real-world skills. Zeenat Khan offers online tuition. Each has three years of experience in what they teach.

Which classes are online and offline?+

Vedant Kumar’s coding and embedded engineering classes and Amritansh Dubey’s real-world skills classes are offline in Delhi. Zeenat Khan’s tuition is online.

Where in Delhi are the offline classes?+

Ask the team to confirm the exact location, timings and fees before making plans. These details are not published on this website yet.

Which subjects can I ask Zeenat about?+

Tell Zeenat Khan your subject, topic and class level. She’ll confirm whether the available online tuition is a good fit for the help you need.

Is The Tins an official school programme?+

No. The team studies at CM SHRI schools, formerly Excellence / ASOSE, but The Tins is an independently run student project, not a school-run programme.
